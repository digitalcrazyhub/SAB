Summary Statement

Sri Amma Builder Pvt Ltd is committed to the highest standards of data integrity, confidentiality, and technical privacy. We do not sell your personal data or trade your commercial project blueprints. All engineering documents, contact information, and consultation inquiries shared through this website are handled exclusively to evaluate, structure, and execute civil engineering and construction contracts.

01

Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines the practices and policies of Sri Amma Builder Pvt Ltd (hereinafter referred to as "SAB", "we", "us", or "our"), also operating as Sri Amma Industrial Developers Pvt Ltd, having its corporate office at No. 4/951, 33rd Street, TVS Colony, Anna Nagar West Extension, Chennai – 600 101, Tamil Nadu, India, regarding the collection, use, processing, and protection of personal data and project-specific communications gathered through our website (https://sriammabuilders.com/).

We are committed to processing all digital data responsibly in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and other applicable laws governing data protection in the Republic of India.

02

Information We Collect

In our capacity as a turnkey civil engineering contractor and industrial facility developer, we collect only the information strictly necessary to provide engineering consultations, site feasibility assessments, structural cost estimations (BOQ), and contractual project execution.

Depending on how you interact with our website and engineering desk, the information collected is categorized into:

  • Personal Identifiers: Data provided voluntarily when you submit a project enquiry or contact our technical leadership.
  • Commercial & Project Data: Built-up area specifications, land survey coordinates, site location details, architectural drawings, and operational parameters for prospective factories or buildings.
  • Technical & Log Data: Non-personally identifiable server logs recorded automatically during your web visit.
03

Personal Information

When you contact us via our consultation intake form, email desk, telephone, or official messaging channels, we may collect the following personal identifiers:

  • Full Name: First name and last name of the representative or individual applicant.
  • Contact Coordinates: Valid corporate or personal email address and mobile telephone number.
  • Enterprise Affiliation: Company, manufacturing firm, or partnership name on whose behalf the inquiry is submitted.
  • Designation / Role: Managing Director, Project Architect, Facility Manager, Purchase Head, or Property Owner.
  • Geographic Location: Site location city, industrial corridor (e.g., Sriperumbudur, Oragadam, Irungattukottai), or state.
04

Information Collected Automatically

When you browse our website, our web hosting infrastructure automatically logs standard technical metadata transmitted by your web browser. This includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address and approximate geographical region (city/state level).
  • Browser type, engine version, and user-agent string.
  • Device operating system (e.g., Windows, macOS, Android, iOS) and screen resolution.
  • Uniform Resource Locators (URLs) visited, referring pages, timestamps of requests, and HTTP status codes.

This automated log data is processed solely for system administration, cyber-security defense, network monitoring, and diagnostic troubleshooting. It is not linked to your personal identity.

05

How We Collect Information

We collect information through transparent and lawful channels:

  • Direct Digital Submissions: When you complete our "Request a Consultation" intake form on the website.
  • Telephonic & Direct Communication: When you contact our senior technical directors via direct phone lines (+91 94444 05505 / +91 93802 43013) or official email (mkrishsab@gmail.com).
  • In-Person Consultations & Site Surveys: When site visits, soil-test evaluations, or blueprint reviews are conducted at our Anna Nagar corporate office or at your industrial site.
  • Automated Server Infrastructure: Standard web server access logs collected during HTTP/HTTPS transactions.
06

Purpose of Collecting Information

We collect and process personal and commercial data strictly for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • Evaluating technical project parameters and preparing tailored civil engineering proposals and Bills of Quantities (BOQ).
  • Scheduling on-site structural inspections, topographic soil surveys, and architectural blueprint reviews.
  • Communicating milestone updates, engineering drawings, and statutory compliance documentation during project execution.
  • Complying with mandatory legal, statutory, tax, and local municipal authority requirements in Tamil Nadu and India.
  • Protecting our website, engineering assets, and communication channels against fraud, spam, and cyber threats.
07

Website Enquiry and Contact Forms

Our website features a specialized lead intake form located at contact.html. Submissions are processed through our secure backend endpoint (backend/api/contact.php).

To protect our servers against automated bot abuse and brute-force spam attacks, our forms implement:

  • Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) Protection: Cryptographic token verification to validate session legitimacy.
  • Honeypot Validation: Invisible fields to detect and suppress malicious robotic submissions.
  • Google reCAPTCHA v3: Risk analysis software that analyzes non-identifying browser behavior to prevent spam. Use of reCAPTCHA is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
Direct Transmission to Engineering Desk

All form submissions are routed directly to our internal civil engineering and estimating department. We do not share your enquiry data with external marketing agencies or lead brokers.

08

Property and Project Enquiries

Prospective clients frequently share sensitive engineering and business information, including:

  • Architectural layouts, computer-aided designs (CAD), structural drawings, and floor elevations.
  • Industrial machinery tonnage, crane overhead clearances, and electrical MEP load schedules.
  • Land parcel survey numbers, patta details, and site topography reports.

We treat all prospective client project documentation with strict commercial confidentiality. Documents shared for estimation purposes remain proprietary to the client and will not be disclosed to any competitor or third party without prior written consent.

09

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a web server. We maintain a strict and transparent policy regarding cookies:

No Third-Party Advertising Cookies

Sri Amma Builder Pvt Ltd does NOT deploy behavioral tracking cookies, profiling cookies, or third-party commercial advertising trackers on this website.

The only cookies that may be utilized are strictly technical and functional session cookies necessary for basic website security, CSRF protection, and load balancing. You can configure your browser to block or alert you about these cookies, though some parts of the site may lose temporary session state.

10

Google Analytics and Advertising Technologies

As of the current date of this policy, our website operates in a privacy-first posture:

  • We do not operate third-party behavioral advertising pixels (such as Meta/Facebook Pixel).
  • We do not participate in ad retargeting, remarketing networks, or programmatic behavioral ad tracking.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) or Google Tag Manager scripts are not actively executing tracking beacons on this domain.

Should aggregate web measurement tools be introduced in the future to improve website usability, this policy will be updated in advance to reflect IP anonymization and relevant consent management mechanisms.

11

How We Use Your Information

Personal and commercial information is used exclusively to fulfill legitimate civil engineering operations:

  • Drafting engineering work orders, EPC contracts, and milestone payment schedules.
  • Assigning dedicated civil project managers and site engineers to your construction project.
  • Verifying project compliance with Indian Standard (IS) codes, structural safety guidelines, and local DTCP/CMDA regulations.
  • Addressing billing, invoicing, taxation, and statutory audit compliance under Indian company law and GST regulations.
12

Information Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal or business data under any circumstances.

Information is shared only under the following limited and necessary conditions:

  • Statutory Authorities: When required by applicable Indian law, court order, judicial decree, or regulatory agencies (e.g., municipal corporations, DTCP, CMDA, tax authorities).
  • Chartered Technical Consultants: Licensed structural engineers, soil testing laboratories, and certified MEP consultants engaged specifically on your project under strict non-disclosure obligations.
  • Hosting & Infrastructure Providers: Trusted enterprise web infrastructure and secure email servers operating under binding confidentiality and data protection agreements.
13

Data Storage and Security

We maintain comprehensive technical, organizational, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal and project information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration:

  • Encryption in Transit: 256-bit Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) protocols encrypt all data transmitted between your browser and our servers.
  • Restricted Access Controls: Access to client records is strictly role-based and limited to authorized engineering directors and contract managers.
  • Server Defense: Hardened firewall configurations, intrusion prevention monitors, and secure database parameters.

While we employ rigorous industry security standards, no electronic data transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be 100% immune from unauthorized third-party interference. Clients are advised to exercise care when transmitting confidential data across unencrypted communication channels.

14

Data Retention

We retain personal information and project documentation only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Inquiry Phase: Information from general inquiries that do not culminate in a contractual agreement is securely archived or purged within twenty-four (24) months.
  • Executed Construction Projects: As-built structural drawings, warranty certificates, and contractual records are retained for the structural warranty period and statutory taxation/accounting durations (minimum 8 years as mandated by Indian law).
  • Server Access Logs: Routinely deleted or overwritten on rolling cycles not exceeding ninety (90) days.
15

Your Privacy Rights

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and applicable legal standards, individuals residing in India possess the following rights regarding their personal data:

  • Right to Access: The right to request a summary of personal data held by us and the processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to Correction & Completion: The right to request the rectification of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated personal information.
  • Right to Erasure: The right to request the deletion of your personal data, subject to ongoing statutory, taxation, or contractual retention obligations.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: The right to readily register a grievance with our designated Grievance Officer regarding data handling.
  • Right to Nominate: The right to nominate an authorized representative to exercise data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, please submit a formal written request to our Grievance Officer as outlined in Section 16 below.
