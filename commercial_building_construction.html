Our Commercial Capabilities

From corporate headquarters and IT workspaces to luxury retail spaces and healthcare infrastructure, our solutions are engineered for long-term commercial returns:

Corporate Office Buildings

We design and construct high-performance commercial office buildings that combine contemporary facade architecture with smart interior floorplate flexibility.

Our structural engineering accommodates column-free open workspaces, high-speed elevator shafts, centralized HVAC ducting, energy-efficient glazing, and advanced server room facilities built for modern corporate operations.

Retail Spaces & Commercial Showrooms

We build high-visibility retail hubs, brand showrooms, and commercial shopping centers engineered to maximize pedestrian flow, customer engagement, and brand visibility.

Our commercial execution integrates wide-span entrance portals, expansive toughened glass display frontages, heavy floor load capacity for vehicle displays, and ample basement customer parking.

Enterprise Facilities & Business Hubs

We construct integrated enterprise facilities designed for multi-tenant business operations, training centers, and corporate research hubs.

Our engineers prioritize sustainable construction techniques, efficient thermal insulation, advanced acoustical management between tenant spaces, and seamless utility distribution.

Hospitality & Hotel Construction

We construct hospitality properties and boutique hotel buildings that unite refined architectural aesthetics with intensive mechanical, electrical, and plumbing engineering.

From grand lobby atriums and commercial kitchens to guest room acoustical isolation and rooftop amenities, our seasoned civil managers execute every element to international hospitality standards.

Healthcare Facilities & Medical Centers

We construct modern healthcare centers, diagnostic clinics, and hospital facilities engineered for patient safety, sterile zoning, and specialized medical equipment support.

Our structural and civil engineering accommodates radiation shielding for imaging rooms, anti-microbial wall and floor finishes, reliable medical gas pipeline raceways, and uninterrupted emergency power connectivity.

Our Commercial Delivery Process

  • 1. Feasibility & Site Assessment — Comprehensive geotechnical surveys, zoning analysis, FAR optimization, and initial budget estimations.
  • 2. Structural Engineering & Statutory Approvals — Detailed RCC/steel structural modeling, MEP coordination drawings, and CMDA sanction management.
  • 3. Substructure & Basement Execution — Deep diaphragm or pile foundations, soil retention, waterproofing, and multi-level basement casting.
  • 4. Superstructure & Facade Cladding — Rapid post-tensioned or framed slab casting, structural glazing, brickwork, and MEP conduit integration.
  • 5. Commissioning, Testing & Handover — Integrated MEP testing, life-safety drill verifications, completion certificate support, and final handover.
