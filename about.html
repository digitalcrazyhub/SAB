Our Heritage

From Foundation to Industry Benchmark

Sri Amma Industrial Developers Pvt Ltd (originally established as Sri Amma Builders in 1993) was incorporated into its present corporate structure in 2007 by first-generation entrepreneur Mr. OK Muthukrishnan. Over three and a half decades, the firm has achieved distinguished success across both industrial infrastructure and premium residential construction.

Environmental consciousness, structural safety, and long-term durability form the core ethos of our engineering practices. As market demands and industrial technologies have evolved, SAIDPL has continuously integrated modern Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) solutions, high-precision concrete technology, and lean construction methods to deliver superior project value.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and technically innovative industrial construction partner in India, setting new benchmarks for structural resilience, energy efficiency, and on-schedule execution that empowers our clients' industrial growth.

Our Mission

To deliver state-of-the-art turnkey civil engineering solutions with uncompromising safety standards, structural precision, transparent cost control, and a zero-accident working environment on every single project site.

Our Core Values

Integrity in every calculation, durability in every foundation, safety for every worker, and total transparency in client relationships. We build structures engineered to endure for generations.
