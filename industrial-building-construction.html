ENGINEERING SPECIALIZATIONS

Our Industrial Capabilities

From precision machine foundations to multi-acre clear-span PEB facilities, our end-to-end civil execution covers every stage of industrial plant delivery:

Manufacturing Units

We design and construct high-performance factory units tailored for seamless assembly workflows, material movement corridors, and uninterrupted production shifts.

Our structures integrate robust RCC columns, high-capacity utility trenches, industrial ventilation louvers, and scalable modular bays that accommodate future factory floor expansion.

Industrial Sheds & Structural Steel PEB

We construct spacious, well-ventilated pre-engineered building (PEB) sheds optimized for raw material storage, assembly lines, and heavy logistical transfer.

Built with high-tensile structural steel conforming to IS 800 standards, our sheds provide maximum clear spans without internal pillar obstruction, roof skylights for daylight harvesting, and corrosion-resistant protective coatings.

Continuous Processing Plants

We develop specialized processing plant civil facilities engineered to accommodate complex piping networks, chemical treatment rooms, boiler houses, and industrial effluent zones.

Our engineering solutions prioritize acid-resistant flooring, chemical-resistant masonry, integrated MEP trenches, and stringent fire-containment compartmentalization.

Heavy Machinery & Press Facilities

High-capacity industrial buildings engineered specifically for heavy dynamic machine loads, stamping presses, and overhead Electric Overhead Traveling (EOT) crane systems up to 35 tonnes.

We execute vibration-isolated machine inertia blocks, deep piling to solid strata, and high-tolerance laser-screed Trimix flooring (FM2/DM2) capable of handling concentrated wheel loads.

Automobile & Precision Engineering Plants

State-of-the-art factories tailored for automotive component manufacturing, robotics assembly lines, paint booths, and precision tool rooms requiring dust-free controlled atmospheres.

We implement advanced epoxy flooring, acoustic ceiling insulation, high-spec climate-controlled enclosures, and dedicated clean-room civil foundations.

End-to-End Turnkey Industrial EPC

We provide total turnkey project delivery from initial site topography and soil geotechnical analysis through architectural design, structural steel fabrication, civil casting, and statutory handover.

Our seasoned site project managers ensure zero-compromise safety protocols, strict adherence to project timelines, and transparent milestone reporting throughout the build cycle.
