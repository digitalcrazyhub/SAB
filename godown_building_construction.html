STORAGE & LOGISTICS CAPABILITIES

Our Godown & Warehouse Solutions

From heavy-capacity manufacturing godowns to automated logistics distribution centers, our civil solutions are built to maximize storage cubic capacity and operational speed:

Heavy Industrial Godowns

We construct large-capacity industrial godowns engineered for raw material inventory, palletized intermediate goods, and finished manufactured product staging.

Our structural designs incorporate wide clear spans without intermediate column interference, heavy steel roof trusses conforming to IS 800 standards, and high-tensile wall sheeting with anti-condensation thermal insulation.

Agricultural & Bulk Commodity Warehouses

We build robust agricultural warehouses specifically engineered for the safe bulk storage of food grains, seeds, cotton, and agricultural fertilizers.

Our civil engineering prioritizes rodent-proof plinth design, vapor-barrier damp-proof flooring, ridge gravity ventilators for moisture dissipation, and airtight seals for fumigation cycles.

Logistics & Supply Chain Distribution Centers

We construct modern logistics hubs engineered for high-frequency freight cross-docking, automated sortation conveyor integration, and rapid container unloading.

Our facilities incorporate multiple hydraulic dock levelers, wide weather-protection canopies, clear internal stacking heights up to 12 meters, and heavy-duty concrete aprons designed for multi-axle trailer turning radiuses.

Temperature-Controlled Cold Storage Facilities

We develop specialized cold storage and cold chain warehousing engineered for pharmaceutical products, perishables, dairy, and frozen export goods.

Our civil team installs high-density polyurethane (PUF) insulated paneling, under-floor anti-heave ventilation systems to prevent subsoil freezing, rapid-roll insulated doors, and isolated compressor machine foundations.

Custom Turnkey PEB Warehousing Solutions

We deliver customized pre-engineered building (PEB) storage solutions designed precisely around your site geometry, racking layout, and operational throughput targets.

From initial soil exploration to final statutory fire NOC approvals, our comprehensive EPC delivery ensures certified quality, high structural strength, and rapid commercial commissioning.

Our Warehouse Build Process

  • 1. Storage Capacity & Logistics Modeling — Analyzing pallet positions, clear heights, dock ratios, and heavy vehicle turning paths.
  • 2. Geotechnical Piling & PEB Detailing — Soil stabilization, structural steel fabrication modeling, and foundation engineering.
  • 3. Substructure & Retaining Infrastructure — Heavy earthworks, boundary retention, plinth walls, and storm drainage culverts.
  • 4. PEB Erection, Cladding & Trimix Pours — High-tensile steel frame assembly, insulated roof panels, and jointed laser-screed floor slabs.
  • 5. Dock Installation & Statutory Handover — Dock leveler calibration, external concrete apron finishing, fire testing, and completion certification.
